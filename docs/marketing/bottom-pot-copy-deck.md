**Product:** Bottom Pot

## 1. Messaging rules

### What Bottom Pot is

Bottom Pot is a search-first job discovery product. People start with a role, skill or keyword, refine the search, review the available listing details and continue to the original employer listing or application page.

### What makes it distinct

1. **Search-first:** Start with the work you want instead of scrolling an open-ended feed.
2. **Country context:** Remote, hybrid and in-person describe the work arrangement. They do not, by themselves, establish which countries an employer accepts.
3. **Original source:** Users can open the original employer listing or application page.
4. **Honest information:** Show salary and posting dates when available. Do not invent missing details or turn uncertainty into certainty.
5. **No account required:** Search, save roles and organise a shortlist in the browser without signing up.

### Voice

Clear, assured, useful and human. Write for job seekers, not developers. Keep headings short, paragraphs brief and calls to action specific. Avoid hype, jargon, generic startup language, forced humour and em dashes.

### Accuracy rules

- There are **10 supported hiring platforms and 3 selected job boards** in the current source catalogue. They are not 13 individual employer boards.
- A hiring platform can host career pages for many different employers. Individual employer boards are separate from the platforms.
- The employer-board registry changes over time. Do not put a live database count, discovered-board count or monitored-job count into marketing copy unless the owner explicitly approves a freshly verified figure and its exact definition.
- Some sources are checked periodically; others are searched through a separate live-search path. Do not claim every source refreshes at the same frequency.
- Do not claim all jobs are live, open, current, verified or available everywhere.
- Do not claim “no ghost jobs”, “100% accurate”, “all jobs”, “every employer”, “guaranteed eligibility” or “remote means worldwide”.
- **Eligible** means available listing evidence indicates that applicants in the selected country may apply. It is not an employer guarantee.
- **Restricted** means available listing evidence indicates the selected country is excluded.
- **Unverified** means there is not enough reliable information to determine eligibility.
- Application links can lead to the employer's original job listing or application page. Do not promise every link opens a completed application form or bypasses employer sign-up requirements.
- Saved jobs, application stages and recent searches are stored in the user's browser. Do not imply they sync across devices or are backed up to an account.
- Do not position Bottom Pot as Nigeria-only. The product is intended for job seekers across countries. Source names should remain transparent, but regional sources should not dominate the brand story. In public display names, use “Jobberman”, “MyJobMag” and “HotNigerianJobs” without the redundant “(NG)” suffix. Keep the underlying source IDs unchanged.

## 6. FAQ (About page)

Keep answers short. Show the direct answer first, then add one clarifying sentence only when needed.

**Q: What is Bottom Pot?**  
Bottom Pot is a job search tool that brings listings from supported employer career pages and selected job boards into one searchable workspace. It helps you review opportunities and continue to the original listing.

**Q: How is Bottom Pot different from a traditional job board?**  
Bottom Pot is built around focused searches rather than an open-ended feed. It also separates work arrangement from applicant-country eligibility and links you to the original listing or application page.

**Q: Why do I have to search instead of browsing all jobs?**  
Bottom Pot is search-first by design. Start with a role, skill or keyword, then use filters to narrow the opportunities you want to review.

**Q: Can I search for jobs in different locations?**  
Yes. Add a city or office hub to narrow your search. The “Where can you work from?” setting is separate: it checks the eligibility information available for your country.

**Q: Does remote mean I can work from anywhere?**  
Not always. Some remote jobs are limited to specific countries or regions. Check the applicant-country eligibility information and confirm the final requirements with the employer.

**Q: What do Eligible, Restricted and Unverified mean?**  
Eligible means the available listing information indicates your selected country may be accepted. Restricted means the listing indicates that country is excluded. Unverified means there is not enough reliable information to tell. These labels are guidance, not an employer decision.

**Q: Where do the job listings come from?**  
Bottom Pot searches 10 hiring platforms and 3 selected job boards. The hiring platforms host career pages for individual employers; they are not 10 individual employers.

**Q: How current are the listings?**  
Source update methods and timing vary. Some supported employer boards are checked regularly, while other sources are searched when needed. Listings can change or close, so confirm the opportunity on the employer's site before applying.

**Q: Are salary details shown for every job?**  
No. Salary appears when the source provides it. If a listing does not include pay information, Bottom Pot leaves it blank rather than guessing.

**Q: Where do the application links go?**  
They open the original employer listing or application page. You review the employer's requirements and complete the application on that site.

**Q: Do I need an account?**  
No. You can search, save opportunities, track application stages and export your shortlist without signing up.

**Q: Are my saved jobs synced across devices?**  
No. Your shortlist and application stages are stored in the browser you use. They do not automatically sync to another device.

**Q: What should I do if a listing looks wrong or the link is broken?**  
Contact us with the job title, company and listing link. Include what looks incorrect so we can investigate it.
