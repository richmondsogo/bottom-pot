## 1. Executive Summary

Following the rejection and removal of Breezy HR (`breezy`) in Step 50, Step 51 executed a deliberative, globally scoped job source discovery audit across **9 geographic macro-regions** and specialist technical domains, grounded in the permanent mandate in `AGENTS.md`:

- **Discover globally, evaluate locally relevant eligibility:** Never restrict discovery to countries already represented in Bottom Pot's ATS configuration, and never infer applicant eligibility from where a job board is operated, where an employer is headquartered, where a job is geographically located, or a generic `remote` flag.
- **Permission and quality before volume:** Public HTTP accessibility (`200 OK` without auth) is **not** redistribution permission. Every candidate underwent an explicit permission gate, and accessible candidates with sufficient live public records underwent a `>= 10`-job empirical eligibility inspection (whereas permission-blocked, auth-blocked, `403`/DNS-failed, or feedless sources were not treated as passing a 10-job sample — see Section 5's Sample-Coverage Accounting table) before ranking on the 100-point rubric.
- **Select at most one qualifying source:** Implement only the highest-scoring candidate that passes **all** hard gates cleanly.

### Key Findings & Decision

1. **Selected Single Source for Step 51 Integration — Pinpoint (`pinpointhq`) (`79/100`, Approved -> Implemented):**
   - **Authoritative Public Feed & Permission Clarity:** Unlike Breezy's undocumented `/json` feed, Pinpoint officially documents `GET https://{company-subdomain}.pinpointhq.com/postings.json` ([`developers.pinpointhq.com/docs/job-feeds-overview.md`](https://developers.pinpointhq.com/docs/job-feeds-overview.md), [`jobs-json-endpoint.md`](https://developers.pinpointhq.com/docs/jobs-json-endpoint.md)) as an unauthenticated, CORS-enabled public endpoint specifically designed for external job displays and custom boards ([`help.pinpoint.support/.../embedding-job-listings-on-an-external-website`](https://help.pinpoint.support/en/articles/17141546-embedding-job-listings-on-an-external-website)). Documented IP rate limits are `120 requests / ~1 min` and `240 requests / ~8 min` ([`rate-limits.md`](https://developers.pinpointhq.com/docs/rate-limits.md)), and tenant `robots.txt` (`workwithus`, `pathify`, `sunking`) explicitly permits `User-Agent: *` crawling of `/postings.json`, `/jobs.rss`, and public `/postings/*` pages.
   - **Real Nigeria, Africa, Global Remote & Technical Evidence (Distinct from Applicant Eligibility):** Probing **24 tenant subdomains across 4 census batches** (plus `3` earlier exploratory failed probes; reproducible census manifest in [`tests/fixtures/real/pinpointhq/probe_census_manifest.json`](../../tests/fixtures/real/pinpointhq/probe_census_manifest.json)) yielded **`17` parsed non-empty `200 OK` boards totaling `558` live postings** (correcting an earlier draft arithmetic typo of `552`), `+2` valid empty `{"data":[]}` feeds (`calligo`, `teachingstrategies`), `+1` `301` redirect (`cintra -> pssg`), `+2` `HTTP 404` responses (`brightsg`, `rowden`), `+1` truncated `2.18 MB` stream (`davies`), and `+1` SSL timeout (`receptive`). Across completely enumerated African-hiring boards (`sunking`, `indrive`, `bizimply`), observed **job-geography counts** (which overlap and are NOT equivalent to applicant eligibility) show: **`sunking.pinpointhq.com`** (`183` jobs: **`36` `location.name="Nigeria"`** + `2` `Africa`-tagged descriptions explicitly listing `"Lagos, Nigeria or Nairobi, Kenya"` = `38` Nigeria-referenced postings, predominantly onsite sales/field operations plus `2` regional tech/design roles; `153/183` [`83.6%`] Africa-geography total *already inclusive* of Nigeria and Kenya), **`indrive.pinpointhq.com`** (`161` jobs: `1` Nigeria, `9` Egypt, `3` South Africa, `2` Morocco, `2` Tunisia = `17` inclusive Africa-geography postings across AI/ML/geospatial/delivery), and **`bizimply.pinpointhq.com`** (`1` `Egypt` remote Senior SDET), yielding non-additive enumerated job-geography totals of **`37`** `location.name="Nigeria"` (`39` Nigeria-referenced), **`10`** `location.name="Egypt"`, and **`171`** inclusive Africa-geography postings. In the **`16`-job manual applicant-eligibility sample**, **`5/16`** are verified Nigeria-eligible, **`4/16`** are eligible for another African country (`Egypt`) without established Nigerian eligibility, **`1/16`** is `Unverified`, and **`6/16`** are `Restricted` outside Africa. No previously registered monitorable Pinpoint integration existed, adding a new ATS ecosystem (`*.pinpointhq.com`), though exhaustive posting-level overlap with all existing providers was not audited.
   - **Technical Reliability, HTTP 404/Snapshot Semantics & Single-GET Completeness:** Single `GET /postings.json` returns complete HTML descriptions (`description`, `key_responsibilities`, `skills_knowledge_expertise`, `benefits`), structured `workplace_type`, `employment_type`, `job.department`, structured `compensation_*` (`compensation_visible` flag), immutable UUID URLs (`/en/postings/{uuid}`) alongside numeric `id`, and honors weak `ETag` `If-None-Match` with **`HTTP 304 Not Modified` (`0` bytes)**. Closure & error semantics are strictly three-part: (1) an individual closed posting's detail page returns `HTTP 404`, (2) a valid complete `HTTP 200` `/postings.json` snapshot omits closed postings (triggering 2-strike closure after 2 consecutive complete snapshots omit the job), and (3) a board-level `HTTP 404` on `/postings.json` fails closed (`snapshot_complete=False`) and never closes monitored jobs. Both monitoring and direct board routing use a unified configurable `25.0s` timeout (`pinpoint_board_request_timeout_seconds`, bounded by `search_hard_timeout_seconds` in direct routing) to handle slow 1–2 MB feeds (~16–20.2s observed).
   - **ADR 0001 Boundary Observed:** `/postings.json` omits `datePosted` / creation timestamps (`deadline_at` is the application deadline and must never be used as `posted_at`). Therefore `PinpointBoardAdapter` and `probe_pinpoint_board` leave `posted_at=None`, while individual posting HTML pages (`{slug}.pinpointhq.com/en/postings/{uuid}`) provide embedded Schema.org `JobPosting` JSON-LD `datePosted` when enriched via `ats_page_enricher.py`.

Best used as ethical **`Discovery-only`** employer-slug intelligence.
   - **`Remote OK` (`52/100`, Rank #7), `Himalayas` (`49/100`, Rank #8), `Remotive` (`44/100`, Rank #9):** Blocked by direct-apply-URL stripping, 24h delays (`Remotive`), commercial fees (`$5k/mo`), or redistribution restrictions.

## 3. Three-Dimensional Separation Framework (Empirical Evidence)

Throughout Step 51, **Source geography**, **Job geography**, and **Applicant eligibility** were logged as independent dimensions:

1. **Counterexample 1 — UK ATS Hosting Direct African Operations & Engineering (`Pinpoint` — `sunking.pinpointhq.com` & `indrive.pinpointhq.com`):**
   - *Source geography:* Pinpoint HQ (`pinpointhq.com`) is incorporated in Jersey (UK Channel Islands).
   - *Job geography vs. Applicant eligibility:* On `sunking.pinpointhq.com`, **`153/183` (`83.6%`)** carry an Africa **job-geography** tag—a regional total that **already includes** Sun King's **`36`** `location.name="Nigeria"` postings, **`16`** `Kenya` postings, and **`3`** `location.name="Africa"` postings (two of which—`593610` `PayG Phones TechOps Manager` and `581464` `Senior Product Designer` in `Global Analytics and Technology`—specify *"Location: Lagos, Nigeria or Nairobi, Kenya"*, yielding **`38`** Nigeria-referenced postings on Sun King; most of the `36` country-tagged Nigeria roles are onsite sales/store/installation roles rather than software engineering). Meanwhile `indrive.pinpointhq.com` lists `1` Nigeria role, `9` Egypt roles (mostly hybrid/Cairo-local engineering/AI/data roles), `3` South Africa, `2` Morocco, and `2` Tunisia roles (`17` inclusive Africa-geography postings). Country and regional job-geography counts **overlap and do not prove Nigerian applicant eligibility**.
2. **Counterexample 2 — Aggregator `EMEA` Label Masking Europe-Only Restrictions (`Jobicy`):**
   - *Source geography:* Global remote aggregator (`jobicy.com`).
   - *Job geography metadata:* `jobGeo: "EMEA"`.
   - *Applicant eligibility:* On **`12/12`** sampled recent `EMEA` engineering jobs (`163127`, `163124`, `163121`, `163116`, `163114`, `163113`, `163106`, `163099`, `163065`, `155731`, plus `163125`/`163122` excluding France/Italy/Germany), actual geo/description constraints pointed to **Netherlands, Ireland, Poland, Spain, Germany, France, Portugal, or European Union residency**—`0/12` verified Nigerian eligibility.
3. **Counterexample 3 — Nigerian/African Curated Boards Hosting Existing Global ATS Postings (`OpenToAfrica` & `RemoteWork.ng`):**
   - *Source geography:* Africa-targeted aggregators.
   - *Applicant eligibility:* High (`10/10` sampled OpenToAfrica jobs open to Africa/Nigeria/Worldwide).
   - *Incremental value:* Zero unique direct listings—`10/10` (`100%`) of sampled `OpenToAfrica` jobs and the inspected Next.js rows on `RemoteWork.ng` link to **Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, and Recruitee** employer boards that Bottom Pot can monitor at the primary source!
4. **Counterexample 4 — `Fully remote` Label Paired with Domestic Residence Lock (`Pinpoint` — `Pathify` & `WorkWithUs`):**
   - `pathify.pinpointhq.com` job `558423` (`Software Engineer`, Python/Django/Angular/TS) has `workplace_type: "remote"` and `workplace_type_text: "Fully remote"`, **but** `location.name: "Australia (Remote)"` and `skills_knowledge_expertise` states `Work remotely from anywhere in Australia` + `Unrestricted working rights in Australia` -> **`Restricted`** (`nigeria_eligible=False`).

### 4.1 Pinpoint (`pinpointhq`) — Selected Source

2. **Source category:** Employer ATS / career board.
3. **Source geography:** United Kingdom (Jersey).
4. **Intended audience & markets:** Multi-region employers using Pinpoint ATS (`sunking`, `indrive`, `bizimply`, `cfc`, `intermedia`, `emergn`, `tradingtechnologies`, `whiteswandata`, `surgohealth`, `confluence`, `scurri`, `coforma`, `pathify`, `workwithus`).
5. **Official API / feed mechanism:** Documented `GET https://{slug}.pinpointhq.com/postings.json` (plus `/jobs.rss` / `/jobs.xml`), returning `{"data": [...]}`; HTML detail pages at `https://{slug}.pinpointhq.com/en/postings/{uuid}` embed Schema.org `JobPosting` JSON-LD.
6. **Authentication requirements:** None (unauthenticated, CORS enabled).
7. **Published usage & redistribution terms:** `developers.pinpointhq.com/docs/job-feeds-overview.md` (`updatedAt: 2026-06-02`), `jobs-json-endpoint.md` (`2026-04-20`), and `help.pinpoint.support/.../17141546-embedding-job-listings-on-an-external-website` (`2026-09-24`) explicitly document using `/postings.json` to display employer openings on external job websites and custom career experiences. `robots.txt` allows `/postings.json` and public `/postings/*` while disallowing unlisted flagged postings and `/mydata`, `/admin`, `/companies`.
8. **Attribution & application links:** Preserves canonical `url` (`/en/postings/{uuid}`) and direct `/applications/new` ATS apply route with full employer identity.
9. **Complete job descriptions:** Yes — single `GET` returns full HTML `description`, `key_responsibilities`, `skills_knowledge_expertise`, and `benefits` (`desc=True` on all 17 non-empty active boards).
10. **Identity & canonical URL stability:** Stable UUID path (`/en/postings/{uuid}`) plus numeric `id` (`"558423"`); `/applications/new`, locale prefixes (`/en/`, `/pt-BR/`), and `/postings/{uuid}` normalize cleanly to `https://{slug}.pinpointhq.com/postings/{uuid}`.
11. **Freshness, publication dates & closure behavior:** `/postings.json` omits creation timestamps (exposes `deadline_at` only; `posted_at` stays `None` per ADR 0001; detail HTML JSON-LD provides `datePosted`). Closure and error semantics follow three separate behaviors: (1) an individual closed posting's detail page returns `HTTP 404`; (2) a valid complete `HTTP 200` `/postings.json` snapshot omits closed postings (with `missing_confirmation_threshold=2` before marking `closed`); and (3) a board-level `HTTP 404` on `/postings.json` is treated **fail-closed** as an incomplete/failed check (`snapshot_complete=False`), never as an empty snapshot or grounds for closing monitored jobs (empty boards return `HTTP 200` `{"data":[]}`).
12. **Pagination, rate limits & conditional requests:** Unpaginated single response (`?page=2`, `?limit=1` return identical payload hash); documented IP rate limits (`120 req/1 min`, `240 req/8 min`, `HTTP 429` with `RateLimit-*` & `Retry-After`); emits weak `ETag` (`W/"..."`) and returns `HTTP 304 Not Modified` (`0` B) on `If-None-Match`. Note: renamed tenants return `HTTP 301` (e.g., `cintra -> pssg`), and larger/slower boards take `~16–37s` (`confluence` `16.0s`, `indrive` `18.3s`, `emergn` `18.7s`, `sunking` `20.2s`, `cfc` `36.6s`). Both monitoring and direct board routing enforce `follow_redirects=False`, a `3 MiB` response cap, and configurable `pinpoint_board_request_timeout_seconds = 25.0` (safely above the ~20s tier and bounded by `search_hard_timeout_seconds` on direct routing).
13. **Geographic & applicant restrictions:** Real mix across boards: Nigeria/Africa onsite, hybrid, & remote (`sunking`, `indrive`, `bizimply`), EU/UK remote (`workwithus`, `emergn`), US remote (`intermedia`, `coforma`), Australia remote (`pathify`). Requires per-job eligibility extraction.
14. **Incremental value:** **High** — no previously registered monitorable Pinpoint integration existed, adding a new ATS ecosystem (`*.pinpointhq.com`) with direct Nigeria/Egypt/Kenya/Africa and global tech employer feeds (`Sun King`, `inDrive`, `Bizimply`, etc.). Exhaustive job-level cross-source duplicate comparison against all existing providers was **not** audited.
15. **Maintenance cost:** Low (~500 lines adapter + URL parser/normalizer + router, reusing existing resilience/registry/monitor infrastructure).

## 8. Pinpoint (`pinpointhq`) Implementation Architecture

Step 51 integrates `pinpointhq` end-to-end across backend discovery, board monitoring, direct routing, URL normalization, enrichment, and frontend inventory (`18` active ATS platforms + `3` Nigerian = `21` total; `9` `MONITORABLE_PROVIDERS`):

1. **Configuration & Serper Dork Exclusion ([`config.py`](../../src/project_files/config.py), [`src/config.py`](../../src/config.py), [`query_builder.py`](../../src/project_files/query_builder.py)):**
   - Added `ATSConfig(name="pinpointhq", site_operator="pinpointhq.com", label="Pinpoint")` to `ATS_PLATFORMS`.
   - Added `pinpoint_board_max_response_bytes = 3 * 1024 * 1024` (`3 MiB`) and `pinpoint_board_request_timeout_seconds = 25.0` to `Settings`.
   - Excluded marketing/docs hosts (`-site:www.pinpointhq.com`, `-site:developers.pinpointhq.com`, `-site:app.pinpointhq.com`) in `ATS_EXCLUDED_SUBDOMAINS`.
2. **URL Parsing & Cross-Source Deduplication ([`ats_url_parser.py`](../../src/project_files/ats_url_parser.py), [`url_normalizer.py`](../../src/project_files/url_normalizer.py)):**
   - Parses `https://{slug}.pinpointhq.com/(?:{locale}/)?postings/{uuid}(?:/applications/new|/apply)?` and legacy `/jobs/{numeric_id}` URLs while rejecting `_PINPOINT_RESERVED_SUBDOMAINS`.
   - Added `parse_pinpoint_board_root_url(url)` so board root hits discovered in Search register monitorable boards cleanly.
   - `canonicalize_pinpoint_posting_url` / `build_url_match_key` collapse locale prefixes (`/en/`, `/pt-BR/`), uppercase UUIDs, `http://`, tracking params, and `/applications/new` suffixes to `https://{slug}.pinpointhq.com/postings/{uuid}`.
3. **Board Monitoring Adapter ([`board_adapters.py`](../../src/project_files/board_adapters.py), [`company_board_registry.py`](../../src/project_files/company_board_registry.py)):**
   - Added `resolve_pinpoint_board_timeout(override=None, bound_to_search_timeout=False)` reading `settings.pinpoint_board_request_timeout_seconds` (`25.0s`, safely above observed ~20.2s streaming times for large boards) floored by `settings.ats_request_timeout_seconds` (`5s`) and optionally capped at `settings.search_hard_timeout_seconds` (`90s`) when called from interactive direct routing.
   - `PinpointBoardAdapter` streams `GET https://{slug}.pinpointhq.com/postings.json` (`follow_redirects=False`, `resolve_pinpoint_board_timeout(timeout, bound_to_search_timeout=False)`, `Accept-Encoding: gzip, deflate`, conditional `If-None-Match` `304 Not Modified`, SHA-256 `compute_board_fingerprint`).
   - `parse_pinpoint_postings_json` validates JSON root, `data` list, required `id`/`title`/`url`, tenant host consistency, and unique IDs atomically — failing closed with `snapshot_complete=False` on any corruption, `301` redirect, board-level `HTTP 404` on `/postings.json`, `429`, `5xx`, or `ResponseTooLargeError` (never treating board-level `404` as an empty snapshot), while valid complete `HTTP 200` snapshots (including `{"data":[]}`) enforce `missing_confirmation_threshold=2` when individual closed jobs disappear from `/postings.json` (and closed detail pages return `HTTP 404`).
   - `_pinpoint_item_to_listing` combines `description` + `key_responsibilities` + `skills_knowledge_expertise` + `benefits`, populates `requirements` from `skills_knowledge_expertise`, honors `compensation_visible=True` for `baseSalary`, maps `workplace_type` & `employment_type` (`permanent_full_time` -> `full_time`) without inventing `posted_at` from `deadline_at`, preserves `location.name` (avoiding city-override bugs like Lagos HQ on Ebonyi postings), and populates canonical UUID IDs plus `pinpointhq:{slug}:{numeric_id}` aliases.
4. **Direct Board Routing, Enricher, Extraction & Frontend Sync:**
   - Added `probe_pinpoint_board` to `probe_direct_boards` ([`direct_board_router.py`](../../src/project_files/direct_board_router.py)) using `resolve_pinpoint_board_timeout(bound_to_search_timeout=True)` (`25.0s` default, capped at `search_hard_timeout_seconds`); wired `parse_pinpoint_board_root_url` into [`search_pipeline.py`](../../src/project_files/search_pipeline.py); enabled `pinpointhq` UUID identity + JSON-LD `datePosted`/`baseSalary` enrichment in [`ats_page_enricher.py`](../../src/project_files/ats_page_enricher.py) and [`job_extraction_service.py`](../../src/project_files/job_extraction_service.py).
